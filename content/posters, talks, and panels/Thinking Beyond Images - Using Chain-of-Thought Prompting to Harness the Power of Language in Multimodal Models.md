# Description
This was my first research project! I conducted it under the mentorship of [Sarah Pratt](https://sarahpratt.github.io/) and the supervision of [Professor Ali Farhadi](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~ali/) as part of the [RAIVN Lab](https://raivn.cs.washington.edu/). I went on to present this work at a few venues (listed under [[posters, talks, and panels]]).
