I started writing this paper in the winter of 2024 after taking my first computing ethics, taught by (my current advisor) [Professor Katharina Reinecke](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~reinecke/). This course was pivotal for me, because it was the first time I had truly considered the harms that resulted from the technology I was developing. The course, combined with my work in the [[Allen School Scholars Program]], motivated a shift in my research toward the domain of responsible AI. This was the primary subject tackled within my undergraduate honors thesis: [[Multimodal Approaches to Fair Image Classification - An Ethical Perspective]].
