title: Providing Supplemental Programs for First Generation and-or Low-Income CS Students in Trying Financial Times
tags: education
---
The idea for this panel came from [Professor Lauren Bricker](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~bricker/#/home) as I was [[teaching/]] courses the CSE 197 series. We had built a wealth of experience in supporting students within the [[Allen School Scholars Program]], so we reached out to educators from other universities across the nation with the aim of understanding how they support students of similar identities.

We were met with a very generous and positive response, allowing for the formation of this panel at [SIGCSE 2026](https://sigcse2026.sigcse.org/details/sigcse-ts-2026-panels/9/Providing-Supplemental-Programs-for-First-Generation-and-or-Low-Income-CS-Students-in). Through this work, we discussed and condensed our experiences, with the aim to provide a resource for other universities looking to begin similar programs.
