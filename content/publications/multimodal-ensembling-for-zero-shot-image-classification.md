# Description
I started writing this abstract in 2023 under the mentorship of [Hyo Lee](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hyodonglee/) as part of the [Google CS Research Mentorship Program](https://research.google/programs-and-events/past-programs/csrmp/). The research idea originated from my work with [Sarah Pratt](https://sarahpratt.github.io/) during undergrad. I later built upon this idea within [[d3g-diverse-demographic-data-generation-increases-zero-shot-image-classification-accuracy-within-multimodal-models | D3G: Diverse Demographic Data Generation Increases Zero-Shot Image Classification Accuracy within Multimodal Models]] improve how multimodal models perform when classifying demographic information within images.

The abstract was completed and submitted to [AAAI-2024](https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-24/) on the [AAAI-UC](https://aaai.org/aaai-24-conference/undergraduate-consortium-program/#program) track. In addition, I turned this abstract into a poster which I presented at the same conference (see [[posters-talks-and-panels/ | posters, talks, and panels]]). This abstract set the foundation for what would become my undergraduate honors thesis (see [[publications/ | publications]]).
