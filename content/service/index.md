# [[com2 | COM²]] Student Advisor (2024 - 2025):
## Description
Support executive and associate officers in developing events throughout the academic year. Lead the development of new initiatives within the student group.

## Events
### Seattle Public Schools Outreach
In this role, I developed and hosted outreach initiatives in collaboration with Seattle Public Schools. Through these efforts, 12 volunteers from UW presented to 577 SPS students in 22 classes for 11 teachers at 10 schools! This was the first collaboration between COM² and SPS, and I am excited to see where this partnership goes in the future.

### Seattle Public Libraries Outreach
In addition, I created the UW's first outreach program between the Allen School and Seattle Public Libraries. This program aimed to provide introductory computer science workshops to learners of all ages within the Seattle area. We recruited 10 volunteers and hosted 4 workshops at library branches within the North, East, Central, and South regions of Seattle.

# [[com2 | COM²]] Social Events Coordinator (2022 - 2023):
## Description
The focus of this role was to co-lead the planning and execution of the Allen School’s quarterly department-wide socials. Within the role, I managed event planning committees for event decorations and entertainment, organized external vendors for food, decorations, and entertainment as necessary, and delegate responsibilities.

## Events
Alongside my co-coordinator, I organized and hosted the three largest quarterly socials within the Allen School (Fall Fest, Winter Ball, and Spring BBQ), while also creating the first iterations of our *Git Good at Git* and *Intro to ReactJS* workshops.
