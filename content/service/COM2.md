[COM² (Computing Community)](https://com2.cs.washington.edu/index.html#) is the largest registered student organization (RSO) within the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering. I was apart of the leadership for COM² for my entire undergraduate degree, as well as part of my master's degree! Within the group, I took inspiration from my [[teaching]] roles and was able to develop multiple novel education programs and initiatives. See more about these initiatives within [[service]].
